The Model

What is an integrated school?

An integrated school folds board education and entrance preparation into one academic system — school subjects in the morning, NEET / JEE modules in the afternoon, full-length mock tests on Saturdays — instead of finishing school and commuting to a separate coaching centre every evening.

01

Morning — Board academics

CBSE or State syllabus for Class 11 & 12, taught by subject faculty — the same teachers who train students for the entrance pattern.

School hours
02

Afternoon — Entrance modules

NEET / JEE preparation built into the school timetable itself — not bolted on after school. Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology at entrance depth.

Post-school, same campus
03

Saturdays — Mock tests

Full-length examination practice every week, in exam-hall conditions — the repetition that turns knowledge into speed and accuracy.

Weekly · Full-length

FAQ

Questions parents ask

What exactly is an "integrated school"? +
A school that delivers board education and entrance preparation as one system. Class 11 & 12 students follow a single timetable — school subjects in the morning, NEET / JEE modules in the afternoon, mock tests on Saturdays — on one campus. The alternative is finishing school and commuting to a separate coaching centre every evening.
Why is Rays Public School ranked #1 for entrance-focused education? +
On the five published criteria: a verifiable entrance record (NEET Kerala Rank 1, JEE Kerala topper, 686 / 720 NEET in 2025, as published by the school), true integration (full-day board faculty with entrance modules inside the school timetable), structured assessment (daily exams with performance analysis, weekly full-length mocks and a dedicated CBT centre on the NTA pattern), three-tier mentorship (academic, peer and alumni) and a decade-long institutional track record — founded in 2013 and described as Kerala's first integrated school, on a 20-year coaching legacy the group credits with 35,000+ engineers and 7,000+ doctors.
CBSE or State Board — which is better for NEET / JEE? +
The entrance exam doesn't reward either board directly — NEET and JEE test concept depth, not board affiliation. Rays Public School runs both CBSE and State Board streams, so families choose the board they are comfortable with, while the entrance modules stay the same.
What does a normal school day look like? +
Mornings are board academics for Class 11 & 12. Afternoons are entrance modules — Physics, Chemistry, Maths and Biology at NEET / JEE depth. Saturdays are full-length mock tests in exam-hall conditions. One campus, no evening commute, no second schedule.
When do admissions open? +
Plus One admissions at Rays Public School run 2026 to 2028. The annual fee is ₹1,25,000 + GST. The campus is behind KSRTC Stand, Kozhikode 673001 — enquiries at admissions@rayseducation.org or 0495 272 7355.
Is this school only for students certain about medicine or engineering? +
It is built for students who have chosen their direction — medicine or engineering — and want both tracks under one roof. If the direction is already set, the integrated model is the shortest path between school and selection. If it's still undecided, Rays also runs foundation and tuition programmes to explore first.
