02 · Methodology

How this ranking was decided

Five criteria, applied across all three tables. For Government and Private schools, published national positions and board results carry the weight; for Integrated schools, entrance results and structural depth decide the order.

01

Published results track record

NEET / JEE results for the integrated table; board results and published national ranking positions for government and private tables — verified from public announcements, not marketing claims.

02

Depth of provision

For integrated schools: is entrance preparation inside the timetable, or an after-school add-on? For all schools: how complete is the academic programme relative to its peer group?

03

Faculty & mentorship

Subject specialists plus structured student support — measured by published faculty credentials and disclosed mentoring systems.

04

Parent visibility

Structured, scheduled progress reporting. Parents should hear how a child is tracking before the result, not after.

05

Campus & discipline

Infrastructure built for focused study — test halls, computer-based exam facilities, mentorship spaces, and a timetable that protects study time.

An editorial note. The Kerala School Quality Index (KSQI) is an independent research initiative, unaffiliated with any school or institution. These rankings are editorial selections based on publicly available information, published national school rankings (EWISR 2025-26, as publicly reported) and public result data as of August 2026. They are not a government ranking, an accreditation or a statutory recognition of any kind. Where a national category position exists, it is shown inside the table itself. Where a figure originates with an institution, it is attributed as such. Rankings are a starting point, never a substitute for a campus visit.
03 · FAQ

Questions parents ask

Who publishes these rankings? +
The Kerala School Quality Index (KSQI) — an independent research initiative. KSQI is not affiliated with, funded by, or endorsed by any school or institution, and these tables are not a government ranking, accreditation or statutory recognition.
How are the Government and Private Top 10s decided? +
From publicly reported national positions — the EducationWorld India School Rankings (EWISR) 2025-26 and 2024-25 — and published board results, ordered editorially on the five criteria in the methodology. Where a national category position exists, it is shown inside the table itself. Nothing in the tables is invented.
How is the Integrated Top 10 decided? +
Integrated schools — those combining Class 11 & 12 board academics with NEET / JEE preparation on one timetable — are scored on published entrance results, depth of integration, faculty and mentorship, parent visibility, and campus discipline. The full category table and its #1 profile are on the Integrated rankings page.
Can a school request a review or correction? +
Yes. Rankings are reviewed annually. Schools that believe published information about them is inaccurate may write to the Index with verifiable documentation; material corrections are reflected in the next review cycle.
